Privacy Policy

App: Hatchi Boo · Effective date: September 21, 2026 (updated for workouts and the new name) · Contact: open an issue

Hatchi Boo is a virtual pet that reacts to how much you use your phone and how much you walk. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: your information stays on your device. We don't collect it, we don't have a server, and there are no accounts.

What stays on your device

The app keeps the following in a private database on your iPhone. None of it is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

  • Your pet: its name, level, stats, evolution history, badges and the journal entries it writes.
  • Daily summaries: a summary of each day's phone use, steps and logged workouts, used to work out how your pet feels and to compare today with your own usual.

Phone use (Screen Time)

With your permission, the app uses Apple's Screen Time framework to notice roughly how long you spend on the apps you choose.

  • You pick the apps or categories in Apple's own picker. The app never learns which apps you chose. Apple gives it only an anonymous selection.
  • Apple only tells the app when usage crosses certain thresholds, so the numbers are approximate.
  • The approximate results are stored only on your device.

Steps and workouts (Apple Health)

With your permission, the app reads your step count and the workouts you log from Apple Health, so your pet can react when you move more. For a workout it reads only the kind of activity (for example running or walking) and how long it lasted. It does not read heart rate, calories, routes or any other Health data, and it never writes to Apple Health. Steps and workouts stay on your device.

Photos

If you tap Save to Photos on a pet card, the app adds that one image to your Photos library. It has add-only permission and cannot see or read your existing photos.

Widgets and the Dynamic Island

The Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show your pet's name, mood, HP and similar details. This information is shown on your device only.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • No third-party services or SDKs that collect data.
  • No selling or sharing of data, because we don't have any.

Your control

  • You can turn Screen Time and Health access off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • You can delete your data inside the app with Settings → Reset, which can reset your current pet or erase everything.
  • Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
  • There is no cloud backup, so deleted data can't be recovered.

Not medical advice

Hatchi Boo is for fun and motivation. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or advise on any health condition.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
