Common questions

Will my pet die or get worse? No. HP never drops below a safe floor, and levels and evolutions are never taken away.

What if I deny Screen Time or Health access? The app still works. Your pet just has less to react to. You can change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my data? In the app, open Settings and tap Reset. You can reset just the current pet, or erase everything including the Pet Book. Deleting the app also removes everything. There is no cloud backup.

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
